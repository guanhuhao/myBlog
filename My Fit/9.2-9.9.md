### Harris-Benedict公式计算
**BMR = 88.362 + (13.397 × 体重kg) + (4.799 × 身高cm) - (5.677 × 年龄)**

BMR = 88.362 + (13.397 × 76) + (4.799 × 176) - (5.677 × 26)

**BMR = 1803 kcal**
